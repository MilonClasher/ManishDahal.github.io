About

about.txt

I started to learn electronics around the end of high school. Since then I build things that connect hardware and software.

I study Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE) at Kathmandu University in Dhulikhel. My specialization is communication.

My projects start on a breadboard. Some of them end as a finished device, such as the handheld device and the bench power supply below.

Projects

p-01

ESP32 Self-Driving RC Car

This car uses an ESP32. I control it with WiFi or Bluetooth. In auto mode, an ultrasonic sensor on a servo scans for obstacles and the car avoids them.

Parts ESP32, ultrasonic sensor, servo, DC motors

p-02

Handheld AI Device

This device sends a prompt to an AI service. You type the prompt on an on-screen keyboard on a 0.96 inch OLED display. You can also say the prompt into a microphone. You can add any AI service. You only need an API key and a source link.

Parts 0.96 inch OLED, INMP441 microphone, BLE

p-03

Arduino RC Car

This car uses an Arduino board. I control it with a TV remote. Two rear DC motors drive the car. The motors and the controller have separate power.

Parts Arduino, IR receiver, DC motors

p-04

Elegoo Kit RC Car

I used an Elegoo kit to build a more complete car. It has a better chassis than my first cars.

Parts Elegoo kit

p-05

DIY Air Cooler

I made this air cooler from stacked tin boxes. A 12 V fan pulls air through wet grass. A water pump keeps the grass wet. One battery gives power to all parts.

Parts 12 V fan motor, 5 V water pump, buck converter

p-06

Two-Player Reaction Game

Two players test their reaction time. The game uses buttons, LEDs, and an Arduino.

Parts Arduino, buttons, LEDs

p-07

DIY Bench Power Supply

I changed an old PSU into a bench power supply. It gives power to my test circuits.

Parts Old PSU
